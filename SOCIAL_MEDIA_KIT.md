**Pre-Launch:**
- Twitter followers growth: Target 5k
- LinkedIn followers growth: Target 2k
- Newsletter subscribers: Target 10k
- YouTube subscribers: Target 1k
- Combined reach: Target 500k impressions

**Launch Week:**
- Email opens: Target 40%+
- Click-through rate: Target 8%+
- Early access signups: Target 1k+ (first week)
- Social shares: Target 500+
- Press mentions: Target 5+ publications

**Month 1:**
- Monthly active users: Target 5k
- Email engagement: Track opens, clicks
- Video watch time: Track completion rates
- Social follower growth: Track daily
- App installs: Track iOS + Android

### Reporting Dashboard
- Daily: Social impressions, reach, engagement
- Weekly: Signups, email metrics, video performance
- Monthly: ROI on paid campaigns, virality metrics
